BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Apple Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories. The Company also sells a variety of related services. The Company's fiscal year is the 52- or 53-week period that ends on the last Saturday of September.

PRODUCTS AND SERVICES

iPhone: The Company's line of smartphones based on iOS. iPhone was introduced in 2007 and has expanded to include multiple models at various price points.

Mac: The Company's line of personal computers based on macOS. Mac includes MacBook Air, MacBook Pro, iMac, Mac Pro, Mac Studio, and Mac mini.

iPad: The Company's line of multipurpose tablets based on iPadOS.

Wearables, Home and Accessories: AirPods, Apple TV, Apple Watch, Beats products, HomePod, iPod touch, and accessories.

Services: The Company's services include advertising, AppleCare, cloud services, digital content, and payment services.

FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS

Net sales for fiscal year ended September 27, 2025 totaled $406.0 billion compared to $383.3 billion for fiscal year ended September 28, 2024.

Products net sales were $253.8 billion and Services net sales were $108.9 billion for fiscal year 2025.

The Services segment achieved gross margins of 73.9% in fiscal year 2025, up from 72.1% in fiscal year 2024, driven by higher-margin subscription revenue.

RISK FACTORS

The Company faces significant competition in all of its markets. Competition in the smartphone market is intense and involves rapidly developing technology. The Company's results of operations have been, and will continue to be, affected by global macroeconomic conditions.

Dependence on third-party developers: The Company depends on third-party software developers to develop and maintain software applications for its platforms. If developers stop developing for its platforms, iPhone and Mac sales could be adversely affected.

Regulatory risks: The Company faces increasing regulatory scrutiny, including the European Union Digital Markets Act, which may require changes to App Store policies and could affect the Company's business in Europe.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS

Fiscal year 2025 was a record year for the Company. Total net revenues increased 5.9% to $406.0 billion. Diluted earnings per share were $7.53, an increase of 12% from $6.73 in fiscal 2024.

The growth was primarily driven by the Services segment, which grew 14.2% year over year, and strength in iPhone sales in emerging markets, particularly India.
