# Apple Inc. Q1 FY2026 Earnings Conference Call
**Date:** January 30, 2026
**Participants:** Tim Cook (CEO), Luca Maestri (CFO)

## Operator Remarks

Operator: Good afternoon and welcome to Apple's first fiscal quarter 2026 earnings conference call. Today's call is being recorded.

## CEO Opening Remarks

TIM COOK: Thank you. Good afternoon, everyone. We're pleased to report Q1 FY2026 results that exceeded our expectations on both the top and bottom line.

Revenue came in at $129.3 billion, up 6.7% year over year, driven by record Services revenue and strong iPhone performance.

iPhone revenue was $72.1 billion, up 2.3% year over year. The upgrade cycle continues to be healthy, particularly in markets where 5G penetration is still expanding. India remained a standout with over 20% growth year over year.

Services revenue reached a new all-time record of $26.3 billion, up 14.1% year over year. We now have over 1.1 billion paid subscriptions across our platform, and we continue to see strong growth in Apple TV+, iCloud+, and Apple One bundles.

Mac revenue was $9.7 billion, up 15.8% year over year, driven by the M4 chip family launch and strong enterprise adoption.

## CFO Remarks

LUCA MAESTRI: Thank you, Tim. Our gross margin was 47.1% for the quarter, which is a new all-time high. Products gross margin was 38.2% and Services gross margin was 75.4%.

We generated $40.1 billion in operating cash flow and returned over $32 billion to shareholders through dividends and buybacks.

Looking ahead to Q2 FY2026, we expect revenue in the range of $88 to $91 billion. We expect Services revenue to continue growing at double-digit rates.

## Q&A Session

Operator: Our first question comes from the line of Katy Huberty of Morgan Stanley.

Analyst: Great. Thanks for the question. Tim, can you help us understand the trajectory of Services growth? Should we model 14% as the new run rate, or were there one-time items in Q1?

TIM COOK: Good question. Q1 benefited from strong holiday App Store performance, which tends to have some seasonality. But we're confident in double-digit Services growth for the full year. The structural drivers — growing install base, ARPU expansion, new product categories — are all intact.

Analyst: And Luca, on margins — 47.1% gross margin is impressive. How should we think about the full-year trajectory?

LUCA MAESTRI: We expect some natural quarterly variability, but the Services mix shift is structurally positive for margins. We're guiding for gross margins of 45.5% to 46.5% for Q2.
